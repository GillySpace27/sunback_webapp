# Five Minutes

The clock ticks softly, marking time's descent—
Five minutes stretch, a fragile, fleeting span.
A breath held fast, a moment to amend
Or simply pause, and understand.

Outside, the world rushes on its way,
Cars stream like rivers, swift and unconfined.
Yet here, within this small and sacred space,
We gather thoughts, retrace the line.

Five minutes: enough to plant a seed
Of wonder in the fertile ground of thought.
To trace the path where memories have led,
Or build a world that's newly wrought.

The pen moves slow, the ink pools deep,
Each word a choice, each phrase a door.
Five minutes hold the weight of worlds,
And all the grace we ask for more.

So let the seconds gather, pool, and rise—
Five minutes bloom, then fade from view.
But in their passing, they will leave
A quiet light, a whispered *you*.

---

*Written in the span it celebrates*
